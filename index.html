Redding, CA

Location

About a 3 hour drive north of Sacramento following interstate 5. (the state capital of California)

About

Weather

Very hot/dry summers with mild winters and low relative humidity throughout the year.

Population

Approximately 94,000 residents call Redding, CA home. Click here for the source

Sports

Redding and surrounding areas of unincorporated Shasta County bring tourism for many popular outdoor sporting events such as fishing, boating, snowboarding, hiking and mountain biking.
